## Italian form over Canadian function

I'm a sucker for a bike with straight, round tubes and classic lines. A bike that looks like a bike, even if its use doesn't fit its intended purpose.

This is my 2016 Cinelli Vigorelli Ellise Magica

![](./vigorelli-2.jpg)

## She's never ridden outside

I got this Cinelli Vigorelli from a former track cyclist who claimed the bike had only ever been ridden on the track.

![](./vigorelli-1.jpg)

![](./vigorelli-4.jpg)

She rode beautifully, but I needed a front brake for the sake of safety and my knees. Originally I was going to put drops on, but I had this Syncros bar and Oury grips laying around in the parts bin. 

I thought I'd throw them on till I found some drops I liked.

![](./vigorelli-5.jpg)

> Japanese rubber in widths you can't even comprehend

![](./vigorelli-6.jpg)

I had to order these 28mm Panaracer Gravelking SKs from Japan because no local distributors carried any knobbly tires in 28mm. It's almost like track bikes aren't made for the dirt...

Shout outs to [Blue Lug](https://global.bluelug.com/) for the Panaracer hookup (they even have [Gravelkings in 26mm](https://global.bluelug.com/panaracer-gravel-king-sk-tire-black-01.html)!!! 😮)
